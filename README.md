# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI 

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee headcount, attrition, salary distribution, age groups, education, and job roles.

## Business Problem
HR teams needs clear visibility into employee attrition and workforce patterns to identify priority areas for retention analysis and workforce planning.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Visualization
- HR KPI Analysis
- DAX and Power Query

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employee Attrition: 237
- Overall Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- Average Employee Age: 36.92 years
- Average Salary: 6.50K
- Average Years at Company: 7.01

   ##                                                    ##   Key Business Insights
## Attrition Hotspot by Department ##
- 16.1% Over all Attrition, but Sales is Critical - Sales Executive (57)+ Sales Representative (33) = 38% of Total Attrition. Needs Immediate Retention Strategy
## Early Tenure Risk
-0-1 Year 59 Attritions (Highest)
-1-2 Yearsd 59+ Combined 
- Employees Leave Within 1 Year of Joining - Onboarding and Manager Connect Issue.
## Salary is Biggest Driver 
-Upto 5K: 163 Attritions (68.7%) of Total Attritions
-5k-10K:49 Attritions 
--Low Salary band = High Attrition
## Age Group at Risk
-26-35 years: 116 Attritions (448.9%) - Young and Mid Carrer talent leaving for Better opportunities.
## Role & Education
-Laboratory Techinician-62 (Highest by Role)
-Life Sciences: 38% & medical: 27% by Education Background.
